What This Product Does
Adults — parents, grandparents, relatives — open an account and make regular small contributions ("drips") into an investment-linked account designated for a named child. The adult is the account holder; the child is the beneficiary. Unlike youth checking, this is adult-directed and investment-linked. Family members can also contribute (gifting flow), multiplying deposits per child account. Fully complementary to InvestiFi (adult self-directed investing) and Greenlight (child spending) — no overlap. Open to CUSO investment, white-label, and commercial partnership.

Why Chartway members want this
  • Parents and grandparents — often Chartway's most financially stable members — now have a meaningful, credit-union-native way to invest in a child's future, instead of using a neobank or brokerage app.
  • Drip contributions are habitual and emotionally sticky — members rarely cancel a savings account tied to a child's name. This is among the lowest churn deposit products available.
  • Gifting flow: extended family contributes too — multiplying deposit inflow per child account without any new member acquisition cost.
Quantified strategic lift for Chartway
  • Membership: Each child account seeds a future Chartway member. At 265K households, even 5% adoption = 13,250 member seeds over the next 15–18 years — at zero acquisition cost.
  • Deposits: Long-duration, low-churn recurring contributions. Estimated average contribution $50–$100/mo × 18 years = $10,800–$21,600 per child account in total deposits.
  • Lending: Members who reach adulthood with a Chartway savings history convert to auto, personal, and mortgage products at 2–3× the rate of cold-acquired members.
  • Silvur Synergy: Chartway now covers both lifecycle ends — Silvur for members 50+ (retirement), LegacyStart℠ Savings for families with young children. This is a rare, complete lifecycle offering.
Why This is Complementary to Greenlight — Not Competitive
Chartway launches Greenlight in March 2026 — a child spending and allowance tool (chores, parental controls, spending limits). LegacyStart℠ Savings is adult-directed and investment-linked — the parent is the account holder, not the child. These serve entirely different jobs: Greenlight teaches a child to spend wisely today; LegacyStart℠ Savings ensures adults are building that child's financial future over 18+ years. They are natural table-stakes complements, not competitors.

How It Works
Verified direct-deposit payroll flow establishes a deposit-backed virtual limit. Debit transactions are converted into credit-simulated data and reported to all three credit bureaus via Experian Metro2 — the same standard used by credit card issuers. The member builds real credit history while spending normally from their existing debit account. Every transaction is pre-funded — zero default risk, zero capital reserves required. No new card issuance, no BIN change, no reissuance. A patented mechanism. Admin and compliance portal covers OCC, FFIEC, and CFPB. Open to investment, white-label, and reseller rights.

Who this serves at Chartway
  • Credit-invisible members: ~26M Americans have no scoreable credit file. Among Chartway's Juntos Avanzamos (Hispanic/immigrant) member base — now at 6 bilingual branches — this rate is significantly higher.
  • Young adult debit-only members: Members 18–25 with no credit history can begin building immediately — without a secured card or credit-builder loan, which Chartway already offers and doesn't need to cannibalize.
  • Debit-primary members: Any member with a direct deposit relationship and no active credit product — estimated 20–35% of most CU debit portfolios.
Quantified impact on Chartway
  • Interchange: At 30% adoption of Chartway's debit portfolio × +20 bps = estimated $640K–$960K in additional interchange annually at scale (assumes ~$3.2B in debit volume proportionate to asset size).
  • Retention: +18% retention among participants × cost to replace a churned member (~$200) = significant annual churn cost avoidance across a 265K+ member base.
  • Lending pipeline: Members who build scores from 0 to 650+ become qualified borrowers for Chartway's auto, personal loan, and credit card products — at zero external acquisition cost, directly feeding Keatts' lending growth mandate.
  • Differentiation: First CU in VA/TX/UT to offer debit-based tri-bureau credit building — a unique acquisition tool for underserved demographics Chartway actively courts.
Velera Boundary — Verify Before Launch
Chartway already offers a traditional Credit Builder Loan (separate product — no conflict). This debit-based mechanism is categorically different: no underwriting, no disbursement, no payment schedule, no new card issuance. Velera's certified scope covers fraud monitoring and core payment rails. Debit-linked tri-bureau reporting is a patented niche — there is no Velera equivalent. Confirm with Chartway's Velera partnership team before PoC launch. If the boundary is clear (highly probable), this is a clean, high-ROI opportunity.
